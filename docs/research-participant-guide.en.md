# Korean Speaking AI Pilot Study — Participant Guide

## 1. About the Pilot

This pilot study is conducted to validate the effectiveness of the Korean Speaking AI
system (KDLI Korean MVP). Data collected will be used for academic research and KDLI
reporting.

## 2. Getting Started

1. Open the URL provided by the operator in your web browser.
2. Enter your **participant code** (e.g., P001).
3. If a **PIN** was issued, enter it as well.
4. On first access, a consent screen will be shown. Please read it carefully and choose
   whether to consent.

## 3. Learning Modes

After consenting, four learning modes are available:

| Mode | Description |
|---|---|
| Free conversation | Choose one of 4 personas (friend M/F, helper M/F) and practice everyday dialogue |
| Speaking assessment (q1~q4) | Repeat reading · description · picture · dialogue mission |
| Presentation practice | Topic presentation + auto-evaluation |
| Reading practice | Passage reading + pronunciation evaluation |

Your progress is visible at `/research/student/progress`.

## 4. Data Collected

- Learning activity (mode, session start/end times)
- Speech recordings, transcribed text, NPC responses
- Assessment scores and feedback
- IP address at consent time (first /24 anonymized) and user agent

## 5. Use of Data

- Academic research (papers, presentations)
- KDLI reporting
- System improvement

All data published in papers and reports is anonymized. Only the participant code (e.g.,
P001) is used; names, nationality, and contact information are never disclosed.

## 6. Retention

Data is retained for 2 years after the study ends. All identifying information is
deleted at the end of the retention period.

## 7. Your Rights

- You may withdraw at any time — contact the operator
- View your own data: "Download my data CSV" on the progress page
- You may request deletion of your data

## 8. Contact

For questions, technical issues, or withdrawal requests, please contact the operator
directly.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. After logging in, I'm immediately sent back to the login screen.**
Your browser may be blocking cookies. Try an incognito window or a different browser.

**Q. My microphone isn't recognized.**
Make sure the site has microphone permission in your browser settings. Chrome, Edge,
and Safari are recommended.

**Q. I can't hear the audio.**
Check your system volume and the tab's mute state.

**Q. Are my data saved if I quit mid-session?**
Yes, data up to that point is saved. You can resume learning later.
